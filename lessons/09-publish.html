Publishing means uploading your folder to a server that's always online. A site with only HTML, CSS, JS, and images is called a static site. Static sites are easy, and usually free, to host.

发布就是把文件夹上传到一台一直在线的服务器。只有 HTML、CSS、JS 和图片的网站叫"静态网站"，托管起来很简单，通常也免费。

Before you publish

发布之前

  • Only publish images and content you have the right to share. Company work usually has its own release process. 只发布你有权公开的图片和内容。公司项目通常有自己的发布流程。
  • Use .gitignore to keep private files out of a public repository. 用 .gitignore 把私密文件排除在公开仓库之外。
  • A custom domain like mysite.com is optional, about $10–15 a year. HTTPS is added for free. 自定义域名是可选的，每年约 10 到 15 美元。HTTPS 会免费自动加上。

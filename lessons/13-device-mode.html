Device mode & screenshots

设备模式与截图

Device mode

设备模式

Press Cmd + Shift + M inside DevTools, or click the 📱 icon at its top-left. The page now renders at a phone or tablet size, so you can check your responsive design without a real device.

在开发者工具里按 Cmd + Shift + M，或者点左上角的 📱 图标。页面会按手机或平板的尺寸显示，不用真机也能检查响应式设计。

  • Dimensions menu: pick a device like iPhone or iPad, or choose Responsive and drag the edges 尺寸菜单：选一个设备，或者选 Responsive 然后拖动边缘
  • Rotate button: switch between portrait and landscape 旋转按钮：切换竖屏和横屏
  • ⋮ menu → Show media queries: shows your breakpoints as bars you can click ⋮ 菜单 → Show media queries：把断点显示成可点击的色条
  • Throttling: choose a slow network to see how your page loads on weak signal 网络节流：选择慢速网络，看看信号差时页面怎么加载

Device mode is a simulation. It's great for layouts, but always check the final design on a real phone too, since touch, fonts, and the browser's own bars can differ.

设备模式只是模拟。检查布局很好用，但最终还是要在真机上看一下，因为触摸、字体和浏览器自身的工具栏可能不一样。

Screenshots without a screenshot app

不用截图软件也能截图

Press Cmd + Shift + P in DevTools to open the Command Menu, type "screenshot", and pick one:

在开发者工具里按 Cmd + Shift + P 打开命令菜单，输入 "screenshot"，然后选一个：

CommandWhat you get
Capture full size screenshotThe whole page, top to bottom, even the parts you'd have to scroll to
整个页面从上到下，包括需要滚动才能看到的部分
Capture node screenshotOnly the selected element, cropped exactly. Great for a single component
只截选中的元素，裁切精准，适合截单个组件
Capture area screenshotDrag to choose an area
拖动选择一个区域
Capture screenshotWhat's visible right now
当前可见的部分

Turn on device mode first, then capture a full size screenshot. You get a clean, full-length mobile screenshot of any page, at the device's pixel ratio. Perfect for design reviews.

先打开设备模式，再截完整页面，就能得到任何页面干净的、完整长度的手机截图，按设备像素比输出。非常适合设计评审。
